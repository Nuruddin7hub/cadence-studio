Cadence Studio Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

1. Data We Access with Google Sign-In

When you choose Continue with Google, Cadence Studio requests only basic identity scopes (openid, email, and profile) to receive your name, email address, account identifier, and profile photo. Cadence Studio does not request access to your Gmail, Drive files, or external Google Calendar data.

2. Where Your Data Is Stored

Cadence Studio is a local-first web application hosted statically on GitHub Pages. Your tasks, time blocks, habits, journal notes, focus logs, and profile preferences are stored directly inside your browser using IndexedDB and localStorage. Nothing is transmitted to or sold by third-party tracking servers.

3. User Control & Deletion

You can export a portable JSON backup of your workspace, switch accounts, clear workspace data, or remove your stored browser profile at any time inside the Account & Storage Vault menu.

4. Contact

For questions about Cadence Studio, contact the developer listed on the Google OAuth consent screen or visit Cadence Studio.
